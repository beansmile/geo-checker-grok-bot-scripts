Orientation

Read this report in three minutes

1 · What we checked

Whether your website can be understood, trusted and cited by AI search and answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

2 · What {{overall_score}} / 100 ({{grade}}) means

It is not an exam grade or a search ranking. It reports whether the site states clearly who the brand is and whether its content can be used as fact — and whether AI can read and trust it.

3 · Why it matters now

Buyers are shifting from search engines to asking AI directly. Which supplier AI recommends depends on whose website it can read and trust.

4 · What you need to do

Most issues are one-off legacy problems: a single focused clean-up resolves the majority. Start with the action list under Priority actions.

Grade scale: A+ 90 and above · A 80–89 · B 70–79 · C 60–69 · D below 60. The score is meant to locate weaknesses and order fixes, not to compare you against other sites.

Scope boundaries and where the thresholds come from
  • This score is on-site GEO readiness (technical and content readiness): how prepared the site is to be correctly understood, trusted and cited by generative search engines.
  • Title and meta description length, number of headings per page and similar items are hygiene checks used to keep the assessment consistent. They are not hard search engine requirements. What matters is whether content can be cited as a block and carries verifiable facts.
  • Checked against official guidance: Google states meta descriptions have no fixed length limit (longer ones are simply truncated in display); structured data is not a general ranking factor; FAQ rich results stopped showing on 7 May 2026 (the Search Console report retired in June 2026 and API support in August 2026), and HowTo rich results were retired in 2023; an organisation's official accounts (sameAs) and contact points (contactPoint) are recommended where applicable, not required.

Appendix · Glossary

Jargon in plain English

Term usedPlain English
GEO / Generative Engine OptimizationMaking sure AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can find you, describe you correctly and cite you when answering questions
On-site readiness / gradeWhether AI can read and trust your site. Below 60 means not ready. It is not a search ranking
Entity / entity mapping"Which company are you, exactly?" Mapping means pointing every name, domain and account at the same organisation
Structured data / schemaA machine-readable "company card" written into the page. Visitors do not see it; AI reads it
Answer absorbability / answer blockWhether the site contains a passage that works as a complete answer, with facts rather than slogans
Open Graph / meta descriptionThe preview card shown when a link is shared, and the two lines of text under a search result title
H1 / sitemap / index bloatThe largest heading on each page (one per page); the "table of contents" handed to search engines; and template pages with no real content creeping into it
Lorem ipsumPlaceholder filler text left in a template — an empty shop sign
canonical / robots.txtTells machines which is the official URL, and who is allowed in to read the site
Recoverable deductionsRemoving these deductions raises the score on our scale. It does not guarantee AI mentions you more often
